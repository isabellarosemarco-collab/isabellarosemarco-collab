### Hi, I'm Bella

I like testing ideas that most people either take on faith or dismiss without checking. Every project here starts with a hunch and ends with the data deciding.

| Project | The question | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| [**Resonance Lab**](https://github.com/isabellarosemarco-collab/resonance-lab) | Can Tesla's 3-6-9, numerology or my own lucky numbers beat the stock market? | The numbers didn't matter. The losing streaks did. |
| [**Market Anomalies**](https://github.com/isabellarosemarco-collab/market-anomalies) | Does the market really do X on day Y? | Dozens of ideas tested. One held up: the night before a Fed decision. |
| [**The Crowd Is Wrong**](https://github.com/isabellarosemarco-collab/the-crowd-is-wrong) | Does buying when everyone panics, and stepping aside when everyone's calm, beat the market? | It leans positive, but there haven't been enough panics in history to prove it. The moon did nothing. |

I write about having a point of view at [bellamarco.co](https://bellamarco.co).
